EMBERFALL

Duel of the Four Winds

Four Winds. One table. Reduce Vex, the Stormcaller, to 0 Life —
ride the Rising Wind, chain Windfall combos, and strike when it counts.

30 Life•24 Cards•4 Winds•1 Opponent

How to Play

  • Win by reducing Vex to 0 Life. Lose yours and the table is theirs.
  • Each turn you gain +1 mana (up to 10) and draw a card. Playing a card spends its cost.
  • The Rising Wind: one Wind rises each turn — its cards cost 1 less.
  • Windfall: play 2 cards of the same Wind in one turn to draw a card.
  • Creatures stay on the table and attack from the turn after they arrive. Click one, then click Vex or a creature to strike. Both sides take damage in a fight; hitting the hero is free.
  • Spells strike once. Some need a target — click the spell, then its victim.
  • An empty deck bites: drawing with no cards left deals rising fatigue damage.

Mouse to play · Space ends your turn · 1–9 play a hand card · Esc cancels
